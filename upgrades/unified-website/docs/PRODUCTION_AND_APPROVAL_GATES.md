# Production, public service and government review gates

Project public name: **CYBER SURAKSHA 2.0**. Internal upgrade: integrated 2.2 Government Evaluation Edition.

**State:** independent technical evaluation package. Not government-affiliated, contracted, approved, audited, certified, or authorized for official complaints. These checks must not be represented as complete until independently evidenced.

## Mandatory separation
- Public: self-help information, fictional scenarios, browser-side scam warning detection, SOC learning examples and verified hyperlinks to official systems. No real personal reports or file uploads.
- Local training: fictional cases, case status, SOC investigation notes and role-based synthetic admin. Bound to loopback only.
- Government service: **not enabled**. Written authority and specific integration contracts are prerequisites to enabling official intake.

## Before independent live public hosting
1. Validate domain ownership; publish operator legal identity and support contacts (the bundled contact guidance is not live customer support).
2. Perform an independent penetration test with remediation, dependency checks, threat model, infrastructure hardening, TLS, WAF/rate limiting and availability/backups/recovery plans.
3. Have qualified experts review each Telugu/Hindi/English translation and verify reporting URLs and guidance regularly.
4. Complete manual assistive-tech and keyboard accessibility testing against applicable standards; publish audit findings and remediation.
5. Conduct a privacy/data-protection review and retention/access-control design if any personal data is added. No real complaints are accepted in this build.
6. Establish production change controls, monitoring, support escalation, disaster recovery, logging and incident response.
7. Obtain appropriate legal advice for India and each new jurisdiction; contact relevant department through officially published channels.

## Before Indian government operation
- Obtain written sponsoring department authorization, responsibilities and procurement/agreement, and follow applicable government hosting, branding and domain policies.
- Align with official GIGW 3.0 cybersecurity, accessibility, quality and lifecycle requirements.
- Obtain a valid security audit clearance by an authorized lab and any required quality/STQC certification or approvals.
- Agree on lawful purpose, privacy policies, complaint handling, citizen support, authorized APIs, accountability and handoff procedures.
- Host in an approved environment where required; do not self-designate a government portal.

## Before FBI or other-government engagement
- Submit a capability statement or proposal through the respective agency's official channel and follow eligibility/procurement instructions.
- Provide independent technical evidence, organizational details, data handling maps, security/a11y assessment and measured pilot results.
- Do not claim invitation, acceptance, endorsement, federal hosting authorization or cross-border personal-data permission.
