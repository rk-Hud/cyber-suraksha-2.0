IF MONEY HAS BEEN LOST

Take action without delay

Financial cyber fraud? Call 1930 immediately and contact your bank.
  1. Call the national financial cyber-fraud helpline 1930.
  2. Notify your bank or payment provider using its verified official support.
  3. File a complaint through cybercrime.gov.in.
  4. Preserve evidence for authorized investigators.

Information from official Indian cybercrime reporting guidance. This website cannot lodge complaints or recover funds.
