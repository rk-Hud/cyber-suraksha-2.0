How impact will be measured

Participants consent to a short training session, complete comparable pre/post safety questions and provide anonymous feedback. Scores are aggregated; no individual response histories are published.

  1. Obtain explicit consent and avoid collecting identification.
  2. Assess baseline knowledge using fictional examples.
  3. Deliver short multilingual safety training.
  4. Measure knowledge after training using equivalent questions.
  5. Publish only aggregated results with sample size and limitations.
A locally imported CSV is not independently verified. Never label a synthetic example as a real community result. No data is uploaded to a server.
