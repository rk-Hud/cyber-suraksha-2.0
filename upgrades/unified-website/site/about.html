OWNERSHIP & PURPOSE

About CYBER SURAKSHA

Independent initiative for practical cyber-fraud prevention and safer digital decisions.

This policy and governance information is currently published in English; translated navigation does not mean the full page is translated.

Project and operator

Project: CYBER SURAKSHA 2.0 — multilingual cyber-fraud awareness and response guidance.

Lead and repository maintainer: Jatoth Ramakrishna (GitHub: rk-Hud). This identifies an independent project maintainer, not a government department or registered public authority.

Purpose: Explain common scam indicators, promote safer behavior and point visitors to relevant official reporting services.

Exactly what this website provides

  • Browser-side rule-based scam warning-sign checks; not a certified classifier or AI model.
  • Educational, fictional exercises in English, Telugu and Hindi.
  • First-response guidance and direct links to official government sites.
  • Privacy-preserving presentation of training assessment statistics when users supply anonymous local CSV files.

What it does not do

It does not accept official criminal complaints, authenticate law enforcement identity, integrate with police/banks, recover lost money, block payments, or send data to foreign agencies. A government may consider an evaluation proposal, but no adoption is implied.
