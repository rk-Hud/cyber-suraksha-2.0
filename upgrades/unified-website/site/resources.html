Simple prevention reminders

  • Never share an OTP, password or UPI PIN.
  • Verify job offers and payment requests independently.
  • Do not install remote-control apps at a stranger’s request.
  • Use official apps and websites, not links from unknown messages.
  • Keep your device updated and enable multi-factor authentication.
