# CYBER SURAKSHA 2.0 — Unified independent preview

Owner: Jatoth Ramakrishna. Independently operated.

Includes a public English/Hindi/Telugu awareness interface; on-device scam checker, action planner, knowledge quiz, safety guidance and twenty official-agency references. Existing FastAPI synthetic-training APIs are unchanged and the previous homepage is available at `/legacy/`.

Account login, citizen registration, real complaints, email OTP and administrator actions are **not enabled** on this deployment. They require a separately verified persistent database, email sender, owner authentication, privacy policy, backup/restore testing and explicit operational approval. No official police complaint is created by the awareness site.

Existing production/staging services and the Windows enterprise gateway are not modified by this package.

See `site/permissions.html` and `site/service-status.html`.
