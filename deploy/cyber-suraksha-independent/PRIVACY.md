# Privacy and demonstration-data notice

The educational form controls do not ask for names, phone numbers, account identifiers, passwords or real complaint details. The quiz and on-page URL checker run in the browser. A separate optional API endpoint checks URL patterns without requesting the URL's content or retaining the submitted string in the training SQLite database. The site deliberately has no accounts, analytics tracking, or real complaint intake.

A hosting or reverse-proxy provider may retain network logs such as request times and IP addresses, according to the provider's own retention and privacy policies. Review those policies before publishing and avoid submitting personal or sensitive information.


## Independent-service boundary

This privacy notice describes the public **awareness website**, not the Flask owner portal or its private account database. Do not enter sensitive personal information in educational tools. For actual complaints in India use [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in/).
