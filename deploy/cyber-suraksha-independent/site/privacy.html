Privacy & data handling

CYBER SURAKSHA 2.0 is an independent cyber-safety resource. It does not receive police complaints, and it does not ask for names, contact information, account numbers, passwords, OTPs, or evidence uploads.

What we process

The website's action planner accepts incident categories, a yes/no financial-loss indicator and the selected language. The link checker accepts URL text. These functions respond immediately and do not add user submissions to the training database. Do not enter private or sensitive information into either tool.

Training data

Example cases and SOC alerts are fictional. They are not actual complaints or police records.

Hosting and access logs

The hosting provider may process IP addresses and technical request metadata to deliver the service. This site does not itself add user tracking cookies or analytics. Review the actual hosting configuration before making further privacy representations.

External sites

Official reporting links open a separate government site whose privacy practices apply. No report is sent to the government by this service.

Scope

Guidance is general information, not a substitute for advice from banks, police, or incident-response professionals.
