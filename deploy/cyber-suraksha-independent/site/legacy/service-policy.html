Scope of services

CYBER SURAKSHA 2.0 is an independently operated digital-safety guidance website, not a Government of India, Telangana Police, or law-enforcement service.

Available services

Public safety resources, local URL warning-sign analysis, anonymous incident-action plans, links to official reporting channels, and clearly designated SOC/case-study exercises.

Important limitations

URL checks use basic indicators, do not visit the address, and cannot determine whether a website is safe. Guidance does not guarantee recovery of money or provide emergency response. Training case IDs are fictional and cannot be used to track police complaints.

Report cybercrime

For genuine complaints in India, use cybercrime.gov.in. For financial cyber fraud contact 1930 promptly. Contact local emergency services when urgent personal safety is at risk.
