03 TOPICS

PHISHING

Suspicious links

A message creates urgency, claims your account is blocked and asks you to open an unfamiliar link.

SAFER ACTIONOpen the service from its known official app or website instead.
₹
UPI FRAUD

Payment requests

A stranger asks you to enter your UPI PIN to receive a prize or refund.

SAFER ACTIONNever enter a UPI PIN to receive money; verify requests with your bank.
◈
IMPERSONATION

Fake authority calls

A caller poses as police or bank staff and demands secrecy or an urgent transfer.

SAFER ACTIONEnd the call and independently contact the real organisation.
