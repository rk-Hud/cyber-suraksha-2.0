CYBER SURAKSHA 2.0

Service terms

These policies cover the independent CYBER SURAKSHA 2.0 website, citizen accounts and support-case service operated by Jatoth Ramakrishna.

Independent support, not a police report

CYBER SURAKSHA 2.0 is independently architected, developed, authored and owned by Jatoth Ramakrishna. It has no institutional sponsor or government endorsement. A support case here is not a police complaint and does not guarantee investigation, protection or recovery. For financial cyber fraud in India, contact your bank promptly, call 1930 and use cybercrime.gov.in.

Information you choose to submit

Submit only information needed to explain your own support request, and use fictional data when testing. Do not upload or paste passwords, one-time codes, payment PINs, full financial identifiers, identity documents, malicious files or other people’s unnecessary personal information. You acknowledge that the authorized owner can review submitted information to respond.

Account and access responsibilities

Keep your sign-in credentials and authenticator codes private. Google sign-in creates or authenticates citizen access only; it does not link an existing password account merely because email addresses match. Do not attempt to access another person’s cases, impersonate an administrator, evade security controls or disrupt the service.

Guidance and availability

Educational tools and warning scores can miss attacks or flag legitimate content. Independently verify sensitive actions through trusted official channels. Service availability and message delivery can be interrupted. Stored case history is the primary place to check a support update; an email is not proof that a case has been resolved.
