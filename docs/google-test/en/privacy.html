CYBER SURAKSHA 2.0

Privacy policy

These policies cover the independent CYBER SURAKSHA 2.0 website, citizen accounts and support-case service operated by Jatoth Ramakrishna.

Tools that stay on your device

The scam checker, action planner, quiz and local safety assistant process their input in browser memory. They do not send pasted text to the account API, resolve pasted links or save your input in browser storage. Ordinary page and asset requests still reach the hosting provider.

Accounts and private support cases

When you choose to create an account or submit a case, the service stores your name, email, authentication records and the information you submit in its hosted database. The authorized owner can review support cases and login security history. Do not submit passwords, OTPs, PINs, full bank details or identity documents. There is no attachment upload.

Optional Google sign-in

Google is contacted only after you choose the Google sign-in option and acknowledge these policies. Google provides a signed identity assertion containing a stable account identifier, verified email and basic profile name. The backend verifies it and issues its own citizen session. It does not receive your Google password, request access to Gmail messages or grant administrator rights. Google has its own privacy policy.

Hosting and delivery providers

GitHub Pages serves this website; Neon hosts the account API and database. Google processes optional sign-in. A configured email provider processes verification and status messages when those features are enabled. Providers can process connection and security metadata under their own policies. No advertising pixels or analytics trackers are installed.

Session memory and retained records

Application session tokens and temporary Google sign-in challenges stay in this page’s memory. Reloading, closing the page or switching language signs you out of this website; it does not delete your stored account or case. Account and case records are retained for support and security review until the owner processes an appropriate deletion request. Automatic account deletion and a fixed deletion schedule are not currently offered.

Questions and deletion requests

Contact the owner through an authenticated support case for account or privacy questions. If you cannot sign in, request a private contact channel through the project repository without posting personal details or complaint evidence. The owner must verify account ownership before changing or deleting private records. This policy does not promise a statutory response period or automated deletion.
