Three common pressure tactics

An urgent official-looking demand
A fine, delivery fee or KYC deadline pushes you to a link. Open the official app or type the known official address yourself.
Money promised after a payment
A job, prize or cashback asks for an advance fee. Receiving money does not require your UPI PIN.
A request for access or secrets
A caller asks for an OTP, screen sharing or a remote-access app. End contact and verify through the provider's published support channel.

Need help now? / India

Money lost to cyber fraud? Act promptly.

Contact your bank or payment provider through its official channel. Call 1930 for financial cyber fraud in India and use the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal to submit your complaint. Recovery is not guaranteed.

Outside India? Use your local police or national reporting channel. The directory below explains each agency's remit.

Trust & privacy

Useful tools. Clear boundaries.

Read the security disclosure policy →Authorship record →
Your pasted text stays here
The checker and action planner run in browser memory. They do not upload or persist your inputs. This site uses no analytics, advertising pixels, chat services or browser storage for these tools.
A warning score is not a verdict
Local checks can miss new attacks and flag legitimate content. A known domain, HTTPS or a low warning score cannot guarantee that a page or message is safe. Verify independently through the organization’s known official channel.
Ordinary site requests still occur
Your browser requests this website's pages and static assets. These requests never include tool input. Hosting infrastructure may retain connection and security metadata such as an IP address. This site does not poll advisory services or send text to a cloud assistant. Do not put personal incident details into URLs. External sites have their own privacy policies.
